# Twitter Collect

Collect and summarize important tweets from followed accounts.

## Task
Gather notable content from Twitter timeline and summarize key insights.

## Steps
1. Review recent tweets from followed accounts
2. Identify important, interesting, or trending content
3. Extract key points and insights
4. Categorize by topic or relevance

## Output Format
Provide a curated list of notable tweets with:
- Author and date
- Key quote or summary
- Why it's notable
- Related links if applicable
